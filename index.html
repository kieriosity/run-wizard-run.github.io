Moonspire Run DX

Sprint through the moonlit ruins and reach the Moon Gate.

Controls

  • A D or ← → — move
  • Space / W / ↑ — jump
  • J — cast a bolt  ·  K — radiant light spell
  • H — down-bolt while jumping (hits the short enemies a bolt flies over)
  • R — restart the run

Scoring

  • Collect shards and defeat enemies for points.
  • Finish fast for a big speed bonus (up to +3000).
  • Sweep every shard and all 12 enemies for a Perfect Clear bonus — the only way to challenge the 6,300 top score.
  • You have only 2 hearts — two hits and the run resets to zero.
  • Beat a high score and you'll add your name to the board.
